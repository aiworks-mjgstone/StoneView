Owner on every job

Leading manufacturer of quality stone products

Small to medium projects, with an eye for detail on every one.

MJG Stone was founded in 2019 on two things: high quality and high levels of customer service. The owner is involved in every stage of the production line.

We offer a vast range of materials, from natural marble and granite to engineered stone and wide-format porcelain. A sophisticated blend of on-trend products in a diverse range of colours gives you infinite possibilities for kitchen benchtops and vanities.

Our skilled factory and install team pair high-tech machinery with old-school experience to deliver some of the highest quality work seen in the industry.

Concept to execution

A friendly, passionate team to guide you through.

  1. Consult

    Visit the Southport showroom or send your plans. We talk through material, edge profile and budget.

  2. Template

    We measure on site with digital templating for an exact fit to your cabinetry.

  3. Fabricate

    Slabs are cut and polished wet on high-tech machinery, checked by the owner before leaving the factory.

  4. Install

    Professional fitting and installation at a price point everyone can afford.
