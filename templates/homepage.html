MJG Stone was founded in 2019 on two things: high quality and high levels of customer service. The owner is involved in every stage of the production line.

We offer a vast range of materials, from natural marble and granite to engineered stone and wide-format porcelain. A sophisticated blend of on-trend products in a diverse range of colours gives you infinite possibilities for kitchen benchtops and vanities.

Our skilled factory and install team pair high-tech machinery with old-school experience to deliver some of the highest quality work seen in the industry.

Sustainable by design

Protecting the planet and the resources we have left.

MJG Stone is committed to finding sustainable ways to operate. From waste reduction to water conservation, we demonstrate this in our company ethos in an ongoing effort to operate and grow sustainably. We care about our team, our customers and our environment.

Complete water recycling

All works are carried out wet to minimise dust, and every litre is captured and reused.

Silicosis compliant

Our operations meet Silicosis compliance standards to protect the team on every cut.

Solar powered

Large solar power systems reduce the carbon footprint of the factory floor.

Emission free factory

Environmentally friendly production, free of atmospheric emissions.
